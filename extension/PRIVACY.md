# Privacy Policy — Caveman Mode

**Effective date:** 22 June 2026

Caveman Mode is a browser extension that makes ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini reply
in a compact "caveman" style while aiming to preserve technical substance. It does this by adding a short
instruction to the message you send. This policy explains exactly what the
extension accesses and what happens to it.

## The short version

**Caveman Mode does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.** It makes
no network requests of its own, contains no analytics or tracking, and sends
nothing to us or to any third party. Everything it does happens locally in your
browser.

## What the extension stores

Your preferences only, saved with `chrome.storage.sync` so they persist and sync
across your own signed-in Chrome instances:

- the on/off toggle;
- the intensity level (lite / full / ultra);
- which supported sites the extension is active on.

These contain no personal information and are never sent to us.

## What the extension accesses

On the supported AI chat sites — `chatgpt.com`, `chat.openai.com`, `claude.ai`,
and `gemini.google.com` — when caveman mode is on, the extension watches for you
to send a message. When you do, it prepends a short, fixed caveman instruction to
**that outgoing message** and then submits it for you. The instruction is plainly
visible in the chat.

The extension only ever **adds text to your own outgoing message**. It does not
read, store, log, or transmit your conversations or the model's replies, it does
not touch the page's network requests, it does not automate anything else in your
account, and it does not access any other websites. All processing is local;
nothing about your messages leaves the browser through this extension.

## Data sharing

None. We do not sell or transfer user data to third parties. We do not use data
for any purpose unrelated to the extension's single function, and never for
creditworthiness or lending.
